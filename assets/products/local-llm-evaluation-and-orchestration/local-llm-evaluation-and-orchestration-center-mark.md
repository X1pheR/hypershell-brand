## Design intent

The center mark represents local LLM evaluation and model comparison:

- compact processor / compute-core silhouette
- three ascending benchmark bars for measured comparison
- validation/check badge for completed evaluation
- dark inset evaluation surface
- light neutral chip casing derived from the same surface language as the Hypershell masterbrand H
- broad calm front-facing surfaces, shallow sculptural side planes and restrained chamfering
- blue/violet/pink/cyan used as controlled reflections and semantic accents rather than as the chip casing material
- transparent background
- no Tri-Spike Arc, Family Template, masterbrand H, text or wordmark

## Reproduction intent

Recreate only the standalone product-specific center symbol. Preserve the processor-pin silhouette, benchmark bars and validation badge. The main chip casing must read as the same refined light-neutral solid material family as the accepted Hypershell H-core: premium, calm, precise and slightly dimensional. Keep the inner display dark and elegant. Brand colors may illuminate the bars, validation ring and subtle casing reflections, but must not turn the casing into a neon-glass surface.

The accepted PNG remains authoritative over later reproductions.

## Family-mark workflow

This accepted center mark is the only center artwork to be used for the next family-mark review. It may be deterministically cropped for transparent padding, scaled and positioned, but its internal artwork must not be regenerated or redesigned. The canonical Family Template remains unchanged.
