# HomeSight Center Mark

## Status

Accepted canonical center mark.

## Family context

- Product: HomeSight
- Parent asset: `../../masterbrand/hypershell-family-template.png`
- The center mark is designed to be composited deterministically into the accepted Hypershell Family Template.
- The accepted PNG is the visual authority. This prompt records reproduction intent only.

## Prompt

```text
Create only the standalone HomeSight center mark on a fully transparent square canvas.

The symbol is a compact, balanced triangular network made from exactly three circular nodes and exactly three separate connecting bars. It belongs to the same premium material and lighting system as the accepted Hypershell masterbrand.

COMPOSITION

Arrange three identical circular nodes as an upright equilateral-style triangle:
- one node at the top center
- one node at the lower left
- one node at the lower right

Place one straight connecting bar along each side of the triangle.

The complete symbol is optically centered, symmetrical, compact, and readable at small sizes.

NODES

Use one identical node design for all three positions.

Each node is the circular counterpart of a Hypershell spike viewed directly from above: a round, shallow sculpted form with three broad internal planar regions meeting cleanly at the center.

The node material is predominantly light neutral, close to #F1F5F9, with restrained dimensional depth, broad soft highlights, and smooth controlled tonal transitions.

Apply subtle local brand-color influence:
- top node: soft pink with restrained violet and cyan reflection
- lower-left node: soft violet/pink with restrained blue-cyan reflection
- lower-right node: soft cyan/blue with restrained violet-pink reflection

The light-neutral material remains dominant. The nodes are polished and dimensional but remain simple brand geometry rather than detailed gemstones.

CONNECTING BARS

Use three separate straight bars. Each bar has a slim rectangular body with very small rounded corner radii and straight end faces.

The bars use the same material principle as the accepted Hypershell masterbrand ring:
- sleek translucent colored glass
- restrained internal light variation
- subtle polished clarity
- controlled highlights
- smooth brand-gradient transitions
- graphic shape first, material treatment second

The bars are visually much thinner than the node diameter, approximately one third of the thickness used by the stronger outer Family Template elements.

Apply the Hypershell color flow around the triangle:
- left diagonal bar: pink through violet toward blue
- right diagonal bar: blue toward cyan
- lower horizontal bar: pink/violet through blue toward cyan

SPACING

Every bar remains physically separate from both adjacent nodes.

Use six equal visible air gaps: one between each bar end and the neighboring node.

All six gaps have the same measured visual distance and are deliberately open enough to remain clearly visible at small sizes.

The bars never touch, merge into, overlap, or sit behind the nodes.

MATERIAL AND LIGHTING

Use one coherent neutral studio-lighting environment.

Nodes and bars share the refined Hypershell material language:
- soft controlled highlights
- gentle tonal depth
- restrained reflections
- crisp anti-aliased silhouettes
- no bloom-heavy glow

The dimensional treatment supports the geometry without becoming the subject.

VISUAL CHARACTER

The finished HomeSight center mark feels:
- precise
- balanced
- premium
- contemporary
- technical
- trustworthy
- restrained
- clearly related to the Hypershell masterbrand

OUTPUT

Produce only the HomeSight center mark.

Use:
- true transparent alpha background
- square canvas
- centered composition
- generous transparent padding
- high resolution
- crisp anti-aliased edges

Include no outer ring, no Hypershell spikes, no H, no wordmark, no label, no background, no border, and no additional decoration.
```
