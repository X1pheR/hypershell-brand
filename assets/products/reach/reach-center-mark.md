## Prompt

```text
Create only the standalone Hypershell Reach center mark on a fully transparent square canvas.

Do not generate the outer Family Template ring, spikes, H, wordmark, label, background, border, or any other surrounding logo structure.

The symbol consists of exactly:
- one central circular node
- two curved signal arcs on the left
- two mirrored curved signal arcs on the right

CENTER NODE

Reuse the HomeSight node construction and material language.

The node is the circular counterpart of a Hypershell spike viewed directly from above:
- circular
- shallow sculpted form
- three broad internal planar regions meeting cleanly at the center
- light-neutral base close to #F1F5F9
- broad soft highlights
- controlled dimensional depth
- subtle pink, violet, blue and cyan influence

The HomeSight node family is the visual reference for this center node. It must read as the same family of dot, not a different orb or generic radio dot.

SIGNAL ARCS

Place two concentric signal arcs on each side of the node.

The left and right sides mirror each other exactly in count, spacing, thickness, curvature and vertical extent.

Use the same material principle as the accepted Hypershell masterbrand ring:
- sleek translucent colored glass
- restrained internal light variation
- polished clarity
- fine controlled highlights
- smooth brand-gradient transitions
- graphic geometry first

Color mapping:
- left arcs: Secondary Pink through Violet
- right arcs: Primary Blue toward Tertiary Cyan

Use softly rounded arc ends.

SPACING

Keep deliberate air gaps:
- between node and inner arcs
- between inner and outer arcs
- between the top and bottom arc endpoints

No arc touches the center node or another arc.

COMPOSITION

The complete symbol is:
- perfectly centered
- symmetric
- compact
- readable at small sizes
- balanced in visual mass
- suitable for deterministic placement inside the Hypershell Family Template

OUTPUT

Produce only the Reach center mark with:
- true transparent alpha background
- square canvas
- generous transparent padding
- high resolution
- crisp anti-aliased edges
```
