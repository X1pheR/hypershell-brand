## Derivation

The Family Template is the accepted Hypershell outer-family geometry with the masterbrand H removed.

The original corrected derivation cleared the exact H envelope `[416,460,837,867]` to transparent RGBA. During later product-mark work, detached alpha-noise components were discovered outside the six intended visual components.

The accepted 2026-10-07 repair removes only those detached components.

The six intended visual components are preserved byte-for-byte from the previously accepted Family Template:

- left arc
- bottom arc
- right arc
- top spike
- lower-right spike
- lower-left spike

No intended arc/spike geometry, color, material, shading or placement changed.
