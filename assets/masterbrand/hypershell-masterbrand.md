Create the canonical standalone Hypershell masterbrand as a transparent square brand asset.

REFERENCE AUTHORITY

Use the accepted Hypershell masterbrand image as the primary visual authority for the finished mark. Match its silhouette, proportions, spacing, orientation, surface depth, material character, color distribution, and overall visual restraint.

The finished result is a precise brand mark: graphic and geometric first, with restrained dimensional material treatment.

COMPOSITION

Create a centered emblem on a square canvas with a fully transparent background and generous clear space around the complete mark.

The emblem consists of:

- one perfect circular ring divided into exactly three separate arc segments
- three matching outward-pointing spikes positioned at 12 o’clock, lower-left, and lower-right
- one centered capital H

The composition is rotationally balanced around the center of the circle.

The three spikes interrupt the circular ring at evenly distributed positions. Each spike sits within a clearly open gap between the neighboring arc ends. These three areas of negative space are visually equal and form part of the identity of the mark.

OUTER RING

Construct the ring from one exact circular geometry.

All three arc segments share:

- the same radius
- the same thickness
- the same visual depth
- the same edge treatment

The arcs are sleek and relatively slim. Their curvature is smooth and precise.

Give the ring a subtle premium glass character. The surface has gentle translucency, soft internal light variation, polished clarity, and a fine controlled highlight that follows the circular curvature. The glass treatment remains restrained so the ring reads primarily as a clean graphic shape.

Apply the Hypershell palette around the circle with smooth color transitions:

- upper-left arc: Secondary Pink #FF2093 flowing into Violet #A855F7
- lower arc: Violet #A855F7 flowing into Primary Blue #3C6CFE
- upper-right arc: Primary Blue #3C6CFE flowing into Tertiary Cyan #22D3EE

The three segments together feel like one continuous color system interrupted only by the three spike gaps.

SPIKES

Use one shared geometric spike design for all three positions.

Each spike is a compact, pointed form made from exactly two broad visible planar faces. The two faces meet along one straight central ridge running toward the outward-facing point.

The construction is simple and architectural:

- two visible faces
- one central ridge
- one clear outward apex
- clean straight edges
- balanced proportions

The top spike points vertically upward.

The lower-left and lower-right spikes are rotated versions of the exact same geometric form and point diagonally outward from the circle.

All three spikes have comparable visual mass and clearly belong to one matched set.

Render the spikes in a light neutral material close to #F1F5F9. Use gentle tonal separation between the two faces to create dimensional depth.

Allow the surrounding brand color to influence each spike very subtly:

- top: soft pink reflection
- lower-left: soft violet reflection
- lower-right: soft cyan reflection

The light neutral material remains dominant.

CENTER H

Place a strong capital H at the exact optical center of the circular mark.

The H is compact and balanced, with comfortable negative space between the letter and the surrounding ring.

Render the H in a light neutral material close to #F1F5F9.

The letterform reads first as a simple, confident H. Its main surfaces are broad and calm. Controlled shallow side planes and gentle tonal transitions give the letter visible sculptural depth.

The dimensional treatment is smooth and restrained. The front-facing structure remains dominant while subtle side depth gives the H physical presence.

Use soft edge transitions and shallow refined chamfering so the H feels related to the material language of the spikes while remaining slightly calmer and more substantial.

MATERIAL AND LIGHTING

Use one coherent neutral studio-lighting environment for the complete mark.

The H and spikes share the same refined light solid material.

The ring translates that same premium material language into translucent colored glass.

Lighting is soft and controlled:

- broad soft highlights
- subtle localized reflections
- gentle tonal depth
- minimal shadowing
- clear readable surfaces

Dimensionality supports the geometry rather than becoming the visual subject.

VISUAL CHARACTER

The completed emblem should feel:

- precise
- balanced
- premium
- contemporary
- technological
- restrained
- recognizable at a glance

Preserve the simplicity and clarity of a professional masterbrand logo while giving the surfaces enough depth to feel crafted rather than flat.

OUTPUT
