# ChatGPT App Icon Family

## Status

Accepted canonical app-icon family.

## Scope

Five ChatGPT tunnel/plugin icons are stored in two production forms:

- `full-size/`: 1254×1254 production masters.
- `max-10kb/`: compact 256×256 delivery derivatives below the ChatGPT 10 KiB upload limit.

Canonical names:

- Hypershell General
- Hypershell Infrastructure
- Hypershell Personal
- Hypershell Development
- Hypershell Health

The full-size PNGs are the visual authority for this app-icon family. The max-10kb PNGs are accepted technical delivery derivatives and must remain visually faithful to their corresponding full-size icon.

The files were supplied and human-accepted before promotion into the canonical brand asset tree. No regeneration was performed during promotion; image bytes were copied unchanged.

## Notes

- Hypershell General carries the parent H-core identity.
- Infrastructure, Personal, Development and Health use their accepted domain-specific center symbols while preserving the shared Hypershell family geometry.
- The unmatched `hypershell-migration.png` found in staging is not part of the accepted five-icon family and was intentionally not promoted.
