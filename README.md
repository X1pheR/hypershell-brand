# Hypershell Brand

Canonical Git-backed workspace for accepted Hypershell brand references and production assets.

## Before creating or changing a product mark

Read and follow [BRAND-PRODUCTION.md](BRAND-PRODUCTION.md).

The required production order is:

1. create the standalone center mark;
2. obtain human approval and promote that center mark;
3. compose the accepted center mark into the canonical Hypershell Family Template;
4. obtain human approval and promote the completed family mark.

A complete family mark must never be generated or promoted before its center mark has passed its own review gate.

Canonical brand decisions and visual rules remain owned by the [Hypershell Brand Identity](https://ignis.hypershell.eu/?vault=hypershell&file=_meta%2Foperating-models%2Fweb-ui%2Fvisual%2Fbrand-identity.md).

## Repository structure

- `BRAND-PRODUCTION.md` — mandatory operational production workflow.
- `assets/` — human-accepted production assets and their sidecars.
- `references/` — canonical visual/reference material used by the production workflow.

Keep review candidates, temporary generation output and status/candidate machinery outside this repository.
